Privacy Policy

Last updated: December 14, 2024

Introduction

ReceiptData ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our receipt scanning application.

Information We Collect

Data You Provide

  • Receipt Images: When you scan a receipt, the image is processed to extract data. Images are processed in real-time and are NOT stored on our servers after processing.
  • Extracted Data: The text extracted from receipts (vendor, date, total, tax) is returned to your device. We do not permanently store this data on our servers.

Automatically Collected Data

  • Device Identifier: A random, anonymous installation ID to manage your scan quota.
  • Usage Analytics: Anonymous usage statistics (scan counts, error rates) to improve the service.
  • Payment Information: Processed securely by Stripe. We do not store credit card numbers.

How We Use Your Information

  • To provide the receipt scanning service
  • To manage your subscription and quota
  • To improve our AI extraction accuracy
  • To prevent abuse and ensure service quality

Data Storage

  • Receipt Images: NOT stored. Processed in memory only.
  • Scan History: Stored locally on YOUR device only. We cannot access it.
  • Subscription Status: Stored on our servers linked to your anonymous device ID.

Third-Party Services

  • Google Gemini AI: Processes receipt images to extract data. Subject to Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Stripe: Handles payments. Subject to Stripe's Privacy Policy.
  • Sentry: Error monitoring. Subject to Sentry's Privacy Policy.

Data Retention

  • Receipt images: Deleted immediately after processing
  • Usage logs: Retained for 30 days
  • Subscription data: Retained while your account is active

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Delete your local scan history (via app settings)
  • Cancel your subscription at any time
  • Request deletion of your data by contacting us

Children's Privacy

ReceiptData is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy. We will notify you of significant changes via the app or email.

Contact Us

For privacy questions, contact us at: privacy@receiptdata.app
