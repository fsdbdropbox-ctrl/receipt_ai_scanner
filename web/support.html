Frequently Asked Questions

How many scans do I get for free?

Free users get 5 scans per day. Premium users get up to 1,000 scans per month.

How accurate is the data extraction?

Our AI is highly accurate for clear receipt images. For best results, ensure good lighting and a flat surface. Always verify important data before using it for accounting.

Are my receipts stored on your servers?

No. Receipt images are processed in real-time and immediately deleted. Your scan history is stored locally on your device only.

How do I cancel my subscription?

You can cancel anytime:

  • iOS: Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions → ReceiptData
  • Android: Play Store → Subscriptions → ReceiptData
  • Web: Contact support for a cancellation link

What receipt types are supported?

We support most printed receipts including retail, restaurants, gas stations, and more. Handwritten receipts may have lower accuracy.

Can I export my data?

Yes! You can export individual receipts or batch export from your history to CSV or JSON format.

Is ReceiptData available offline?

The scanning feature requires an internet connection for AI processing. Your history is stored locally and accessible offline.
